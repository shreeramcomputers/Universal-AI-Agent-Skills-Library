# Website, Software and Application Page Standard

Create only pages required by the actual product, jurisdiction, audience, payment model, data processing, and business model. Do not create empty boilerplate pages merely to satisfy a checklist.

## Required discovery
Confirm legal entity/operator, country/state, product type, audience/age limits, data collected, analytics/cookies, payments, subscriptions, refunds/cancellations, delivery/fulfillment, support channel, third-party processors, and applicable sector rules.

## Conditional page catalogue
| Page | When required |
|---|---|
| About | When users need operator/product identity or background |
| Contact / Support | Whenever users need a support or complaint channel |
| Privacy Policy | When personal data is collected/processed; describe actual practices |
| Terms of Service | When users create accounts, use a service, or accept contractual rules |
| Disclaimer | Only for material limitations, informational content, or regulated claims |
| Refund / Cancellation Policy | When fees, orders, subscriptions, bookings, or refundable services exist |
| Payment Policy | When payment methods, timing, settlement, failed payments, or charges need separate explanation; otherwise include in Terms/Checkout |
| Shipping / Delivery Policy | When physical or digital goods/services are delivered under defined terms |
| Cookie Policy / Preferences | When non-essential cookies or similar tracking are used and disclosures/consent apply |
| Legal / Notices | When statutory notices, licenses, company details, or jurisdiction-specific disclosures require a dedicated page |

## Shared content strategy
Use one canonical policy source per operator/business. For multiple products under the same legal operator, maintain shared policy content as a versioned component or shared route only when the legal entity, jurisdiction, data practices, and commercial terms are genuinely the same. Product-specific differences belong in clearly identified addenda. Do not share policies across unrelated clients or legal entities.

## Implementation
- Maintain a page/content matrix with requirement, owner, source, last legal review, and route.
- Reuse common layout, footer links, policy components, and approved legal text.
- Keep policy content editable and versioned; record effective date and prior versions.
- Ensure each page describes real product behavior. No invented addresses, registrations, certifications, refund rights, payment methods, or legal claims.
- Have qualified local counsel review consequential legal documents. This guide is engineering guidance, not legal advice.
