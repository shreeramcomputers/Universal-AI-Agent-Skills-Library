# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We aim to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming regardless of experience, background, identity, or chosen tools.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent while still challenging claims with evidence.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Respect privacy, licenses, consent, and attribution.
- Keep discussions focused and avoid harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, doxxing, and unwanted contact.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, spam, deliberate disruption, and misuse of project resources are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close content; issue warnings; restrict participation; or block contributors for behavior that violates this code. Reports should be sent privately to repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not post private or security-sensitive evidence in public.

## Scope
This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
